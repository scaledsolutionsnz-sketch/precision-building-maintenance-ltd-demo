About us

A Hāwera crew that takes pride in the finish

Precision Building & Maintenance is based in Hāwera, with three qualified carpenters and a second-year apprentice who have 35 years of experience between them. Renovations and new builds are what they specialise in, and they look after property maintenance too, so your place stays up to scratch.

Lately that has meant reinstating a heritage verandah on Hāwera's main street and building the new community core boards for Hāwera's parks.

FAQ

Before you call

Anything else, ring Jared on 027 855 4981.

What areas do you cover?

The team is based on Dives Avenue in Hāwera and works across South Taranaki, Stratford, New Plymouth and Whanganui. Further out? Give Jared a call and ask.

Do you take on small jobs?

Yes. Alongside new builds and renovations they do property maintenance: spouting, doors, window sills, fences, gates and repairs.

Can you help with planning and consent?

Planning and consent is one of the services Precision offers. Talk it through with Jared when you call and he'll explain what your job needs.

Do you do building reports?

Yes, building reports and inspections are on the list, handy when you're buying a house. Call to talk about timing and what you need covered.

How do I get a quote?

Call or email with your address, what you'd like done and a few photos. Jared will come out to have a look and talk you through it.
